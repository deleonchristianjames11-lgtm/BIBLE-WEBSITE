Finding Peace in Trust
Daily Devotional Sample

Key Passage: Proverbs 3:5-6 — “Trust in Yahweh with all your heart, and don’t lean on your own understanding.”

Life often brings situations where we cannot see the complete outcome. When we try to control every variable using our own understanding, anxiety is usually the result.

God calls us to surrender our tight grip on the future. Trusting Him means acknowledging that His perspective is wider and His guidance is faithful.

Reflection Questions:

  • What situation in your life right now requires you to trust God instead of your own plan?
  • How can you practice letting go of anxiety today?
